Signature and technical evidence
RSA-2048 is a conventional laboratory baseline balancing computation and security, not a guarantee for every long-term use. PSS uses SHA-256 and a random 32-byte salt. Neither the private key nor account credentials appear here.

 

 
What does “valid” actually prove?
Integrity and key relationship. The exact text, drawing and signed metadata match the signature under this public key. A changed document or unrelated public key fails verification.
Not independent proof of identity. The portal records the authenticated teacher account that published the notice and stores its public key. That is application provenance, dependent on account and database security, not a trusted identity certificate or proof of who was physically at the keyboard.
Signing is not encryption. Encryption uses the recipient’s public key to hide a message; the private key decrypts. Signing uses a private key to create a signature; the public key verifies. Everyone authorized to read this notice can read its text.
