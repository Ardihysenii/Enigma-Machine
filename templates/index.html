    01 / SECURITY REQUIREMENTS & PORTAL CONTROLS

    College security measures

    Turn controls on or off, click Apply, and test the signed-in college portal. Python enforces each active rule against the saved fictional records.

    Open signed-in college portal →

    Educational demonstration only. These switches affect ALL signed-in fictional accounts. Disabling a rule can expose or allow changes to saved grades. Accounts, grades and rules persist in PostgreSQL. Sign-in, CSRF checks and administrator account management stay protected.

    One account portal. Real before-and-after tests.

    Open the college portal and sign in with a fictional student or professor account. There is no identity switcher. Apply rules here, then test Grades, Professor area and Access history in that same portal. The selected rules are shared across accounts and browser sessions.

    ASSETS TO PROTECT

    Student identifiers, enrolled courses, saved grades, session credentials and access history. Use fictional data only; switching controls off intentionally exposes weaknesses.

    ROLE BOUNDARIES

    Students normally read their own grades. Professors normally manage assigned courses. Administrators manage roles and enrollment. Task 01 changes demonstration permissions, never account roles or administrator privileges.

    Collect actual server responses and before/after records. A selected switch is not proof that a test passed.
